# Competitor Research Baseline
Research date: 2026-10-02. Directional desk research, not an exhaustive verified audit of current prices, reviews, or every platform. Re-check official sites and app stores before launch claims.

## Category map
- Personal AI voice notes: Voicenotes
- Voice-to-clean-text: AudioPen
- Meeting transcription/summaries: Otter.ai
- Meeting assistant/workflows: Fireflies.ai
- AI meeting notes: Granola
- Native recording: Apple Voice Memos, Google Recorder
- General note/workspace AI: Notion AI
- Task execution: Todoist

## Qualitative comparison
| Product | Core category/value to investigate | Strengths to study | Potential hypothesis for Voice-to-Work |
|---|---|---|---|
| Voicenotes (https://voicenotes.com/) | AI voice notes, recall and interaction | Voice-first capture, retrieval | Put approved tasks and follow-through at the center |
| AudioPen (https://audiopen.ai/) | Convert spoken thoughts to cleaner text | Clear, focused transformation | Preserve transcript and extract traceable actions |
| Otter.ai (https://otter.ai/) | Transcription and meeting notes | Transcript, meeting context, summaries | Personal notes outside meetings |
| Fireflies.ai (https://fireflies.ai/) | Meeting assistant and conversation intelligence | Integrations and meeting workflows | Avoid meeting-bot complexity |
| Granola (https://www.granola.ai/) | AI-assisted meeting notes | Human + AI note workflow | Optimize spontaneous personal capture |
| Apple Voice Memos (https://support.apple.com/guide/voice-memos/welcome/ios) / Google Recorder (https://recorder.google.com/) | Native recording | Low-friction recording/playback | Add cross-note action extraction |
| Notion AI (https://www.notion.so/product/ai) | General notes/workspace | Flexible workspace and AI | Fewer setup choices, faster capture-to-action |
| Todoist (https://todoist.com/) | Task execution | Mature task/reminder system | Generate tasks from speech with source context |

This table is a landscape map, not a claim that listed features are unique or fully verified.

## Audit each product using same test
1. Time to first recording; 2. raw transcript vs rewritten text; 3. search across notes; 4. task extraction and traceability; 5. reminders; 6. platforms; 7. free/paid limits; 8. correction UX; 9. export/delete; 10. AI/privacy disclosure; 11. recent negative reviews; 12. what users repeatedly do with it.

## Pricing audit protocol
Use official pricing pages on the audit date. Record region/currency, monthly and annual plans, included minutes, AI limits, platform differences, export limits, cancellation. Mark unknown as unknown; do not reuse stale blog prices.

## Review mining protocol
Sample recent App Store/Play Store and relevant community reviews, ideally last 90 days. Classify capture reliability, transcription, hallucinations, search, sync, privacy, price/limits, export/lock-in, support, UX complexity. Link sources, use short quotes, and don't treat a handful of reviews as representative.

## Positioning hypothesis
“Voice notes that turn into actual work”: capture first; AI proposes and user decides; tasks remain linked to source; no complex workspace; reminders close the loop. Validate whether this is meaningfully better than existing products before claiming differentiation.
