# Product Context
## Product
Working name: Voice-to-Work. Promise: capture a thought by voice, turn it into clear next actions, and help the user follow through.

## Hypothesis
People capture voice notes because speaking is faster than typing, but raw recordings are hard to revisit, search, and convert into completed work. The product hypothesis is that a focused capture-to-action loop is more useful than another pile of recordings. Validate this with interviews and beta data; do not treat it as proven.

## Initial user
A busy individual who thinks out loud while walking, commuting, working, or switching contexts. Candidate interview segments: developers, founders, freelancers, students, creators, and people who already use voice memos but rarely revisit them.

## Core journey
Record -> upload privately -> transcribe -> extract candidate tasks -> user reviews/edits/rejects/approves -> tasks are saved -> user completes tasks and optionally sets reminders.

## Principles
- Capture first, organize second.
- AI suggestions are drafts until approved.
- Preserve the transcript; do not silently replace it with cleaned-up prose.
- Never invent deadlines, people, commitments, or tasks.
- Private by default; audio/transcripts may contain sensitive content.
- Do not require a complex productivity system before first value.
- Avoid integrations until user evidence justifies them.

## Questions to validate
Do users primarily want tasks or searchable notes? Is approval a helpful trust step? Which capture context matters? What drives repeat use? What retention controls do users expect? Are they comfortable sending audio to third-party AI providers?
